Ortak prompt: Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: additional demo e-commerce gallery photograph. Input image is the product identity reference. Photorealistic soft studio lighting, square composition. Match the reference product exactly, do not add logos, watermarks or promotional text. This is a new alternate photograph, not a collage.

Ürün promptları:
- Cardigan: Preserve the same beige oversized knitted cardigan with V neck, three beige buttons and two front patch pockets. Photograph it laid flat on a light neutral surface, gently angled, full garment visible. No model, no extra garments.
- Serum: Preserve the same amber serum bottle, white dropper, gold collar and AURORA BOTANICA label. Photograph it from a gentle side angle on a pale stone bathroom shelf. Do not invent packaging or change label text.
- Phone stand: Preserve the same wooden phone stand and phone seen in the reference desk scene. Make the same stand the main subject in a close three-quarter side view on the same oak desk, with the screen and background out of focus. Show its base and support clearly.
- Cardholder: Preserve the same dark brown leather cardholder, cream stitching and proportions. Photograph it from a different low three-quarter angle on a light neutral textured table, with its existing slots visible. No branding added.
